This dashboard displays current economic indicators. All charts update automatically based on official data.

## Germany

- [**The German Business Cycle in 6 Charts**](/en/dashboard/)  
  Gross Domestic Product (production and expenditure), new orders, production & retail sales, current account balance, and unemployment & inflation.

- [**The Leading Indicator Economic Tension Number**](/en/spannungszahl/)  
  Leading business cycle indicator for Germany based on reported job vacancies, unemployment, and short-time work according to Claus Köhler.

- [**Business Cycle and Labor Market in Germany Based on the “Automated Business Cycle Dashboard” by Pascal Michaillat, Emmanuel Saez**](/en/konjunktur-arbeitsmarkt/)<br>
  Unemployment and vacancy rates, labor market tightness, Beveridge curve, full-employment rate of unemployment (FERU), and recession indicator and probability.

- [**Focus: Industry / Manufacturing**](/en/schwerpunkt-industrie/)<br>
  In-depth sector data of employees, turnover, hours worked, and wages in manufacturing by economic sector.
