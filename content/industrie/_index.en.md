Background: To comprehensively assess structural change in German industry, looking at employment figures alone is not enough. Updated automatically on a monthly basis, the following charts compare year-on-year changes in turnover, hours worked, employees, and gross wages and salaries per employee. The economic sectors are sorted in descending order by current employment (largest industries first).
