Potential output is a central concept in economic theory and economic policy. The prevailing calculation or estimation of potential output is unsatisfactory (see also Thorsten Hild, *Vorschlag zur Bestimmung des Produktionspotenzials im Rahmen der grundgesetzlich verankerten Schuldenregel*, [PDF page 75](https://www.bundeswirtschaftsministerium.de/Redaktion/DE/Downloads/P-R/potenzialschaetzung-und-produktionsluecken-der-bundesregierung-appendix.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1#page=75)). The political handling of this indicator is also problematic: for instance, discussions predominantly focus on the growth rate of potential output rather than its capacity utilization. As a result, economic policy loses orientation, carrying the risk of adverse developments in key macroeconomic variables such as unemployment and public finances.

On the method preferred and applied here by **Claus Köhler** — former member of the German Council of Economic Experts and of the Executive Board of the Deutsche Bundesbank — for calculating potential output, see:

* **a)** Thorsten Hild, *Vorschlag zur Bestimmung des Produktionspotenzials im Rahmen der grundgesetzlich verankerten Schuldenregel*, [PDF page 75](https://www.bundeswirtschaftsministerium.de/Redaktion/DE/Downloads/P-R/potenzialschaetzung-und-produktionsluecken-der-bundesregierung-appendix.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1#page=75)
* **b)** Claus Köhler, *Orientierungshilfen für die Wirtschaftspolitik*, Publications of the Institute for Empirical Economic Research (IEW), Vol. 41, 2004
* **c)** Claus Köhler, *Wirtschaftspolitische Ziele in der globalen Welt*, Volkswirtschaftliche Schriften (VWS), Vol. 564, 2013
* **d)** Claus Köhler, *Wirtschaftspolitisches Umdenken in der globalen Welt*, Volkswirtschaftliche Schriften (VWS), Vol. 570, 2019

See also on the life and work of Claus Köhler: [Duncker & Humblot – Claus Köhler](https://www.duncker-humblot.de/person/claus-koehler-5997/)

## Potential Output, Capacity Utilization of Potential Output, Appropriate/Inappropriate Economic Growth in the Long Term

### Potential Output (in EUR bn) {#potential-output-long}

[Open full-screen chart (PNG)](/produktionspotenzial/01_potenzial_lang_en.png)

**Construction & Sources:** Potential output is calculated following Claus Köhler by multiplying the potential labor force (persons in employment plus registered unemployed persons; extended variant additionally including cyclical short-time workers) by actual labor productivity per person employed (real GDP divided by persons in employment). Annual values up to the last completed year and annualized quarterly values for the current year. Source: Federal Statistical Office (Destatis GENESIS-Online, Tables 81000-0001, 81000-0002, 81000-0011, 81000-0012, 13211-0001, 13211-0002) and Federal Employment Agency.

**Construction & Sources:** Starting from the unemployment rate in January of the current year, the chart shows the annual reduction path of the unemployment rate by 1.0 percentage point per year until reaching the full-employment threshold (≤ 3.0%), along with the necessary real GDP growth rate according to Claus Köhler (wY<sub>r, necessary</sub> = wY* − w(1 + UR)).
