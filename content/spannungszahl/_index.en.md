---
title: "The Leading Indicator Economic Tension Number"
description: "Leading business cycle indicator for Germany based on reported job vacancies, unemployment, and short-time work."
---

The Economic Tension Number measures the number of reported job vacancies (labor demand) minus the number of unemployed persons (labor supply) minus the same difference from the prior-year period. It was developed by Claus Köhler, former member of the German Council of Economic Experts and the Executive Board of the Deutsche Bundesbank. Shaded in gray are German recession and slowdown phases based on the business cycle dating of the German Council of Economic Experts and the Bundesbank (2008/09 financial crisis, 2012/13 euro crisis, 2020 COVID-19 pandemic, and the three-year stagnation and slowdown phase from late 2022 to late 2025). Shaded in light blue are German recession phases based on negative year-on-year growth rates of real GDP.

*For a detailed discussion of the Economic Tension Number, see:* Claus Köhler, *Die Spannungszahl – Ein Indikator der gesamtwirtschaftlichen Entwicklung*, Working Papers of the Institute for Empirical Economic Research, Vol. 10, 1993.
