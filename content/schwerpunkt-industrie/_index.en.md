In-depth, monthly automatically updated time series and sector comparisons for German manufacturing.

- [**Employees in Manufacturing by Economic Sector**](/en/beschaeftigte/)<br>
  Employment trends since January 2019 by economic sector (sorted in descending order by current employment).

- [**Turnover, Hours Worked, Employees, Wages in Manufacturing by Economic Sector**](/en/industrie/)<br>
  Comparison of year-on-year changes in turnover, hours worked, employees, and gross wages and salaries per employee by economic sector.

- [**Capacity Utilisation in Manufacturing**](/en/kapazitaetsauslastung/)<br>
  Quarterly development of capacity utilisation since Q1 2019 compared to the long-term mean (Q1 1991 – Q4 2019).

- [**Foreign Trade Total and by Selected Manufacturing Product Groups**](/en/aussenhandel-industrie/)<br>
  Monthly development of exports, imports and trade balances overall and for motor vehicles, steel and chemicals (World and China).
