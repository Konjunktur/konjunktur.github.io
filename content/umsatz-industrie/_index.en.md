Monthly development of total turnover, domestic turnover, and foreign turnover of local units in German manufacturing since January 2019 (unadjusted data, in bn €). For the most recent reporting month, the shares of foreign turnover and domestic turnover in total turnover (in %) are reported directly in each chart. The selected sectors (WZ 2008) correspond to the commodity groups presented in *Foreign Trade Total and by Selected Manufacturing Product Groups* to allow direct comparison.
