---

This section shows the monthly development of **total turnover**, **domestic turnover**, and **foreign turnover** in German manufacturing (**Section C of WZ 2008**) as well as in selected key industrial product groups from **January 2019** to the latest available reporting month.

Directly below each level chart (in **EUR billion**, including the current foreign and domestic turnover shares in %), a matching chart shows the **year-on-year percentage change** of the exact same series. Shaded areas indicate recession and economic weakness phases according to the German Council of Economic Experts (GCEE) / Deutsche Bundesbank (grey) as well as quarters with negative year-on-year real GDP growth (light blue).
